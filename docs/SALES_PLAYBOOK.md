# MAVORA — First-customer sales playbook

## Target segment
Start with independent electronics and mobile-accessory retailers in Morocco. Do not market to every business type until the product is validated in one segment.

## Positioning
MAVORA aims to help a shop owner keep product information, stock and order workflows organized in one place, with a future catalog-grounded assistant and WhatsApp workflow.

Do not claim cloud sync, live AI replies, WhatsApp automation, payment processing, or multi-user accounts until each is deployed and tested.

## Discovery questions
1. How many products and variants do you manage?
2. Where are prices and stock currently recorded?
3. How do you know when a product is nearly out of stock?
4. Where do customer orders arrive (shop, calls, WhatsApp, Instagram)?
5. What mistakes or delays cost you the most time or money?
6. How many people need access to product/order data?
7. What would make you pay monthly for a tool like this?

## Pilot offer
Offer a clearly bounded setup service: product catalog cleanup/import, basic stock setup, one training session and a defined support window. Quote separately for extra products, image editing, integrations and custom work. The suggested 299 MAD setup price is only a hypothesis; validate actual labor and costs before committing.

## Demo structure
- Start with the merchant's problem, not a feature tour.
- Show adding and searching a sample product.
- Show low-stock state and a sample order using test data.
- Explain current local-only limitation honestly.
- Ask the merchant to describe one real workflow they would want automated.
- End with a pilot interview or waitlist request; do not collect sensitive data in the public prototype.

## Objections
- "I already use WhatsApp": explain that MAVORA is intended to organize catalog/stock/order data around customer conversations, not replace WhatsApp.
- "I use a notebook": quantify errors/time first; do not pressure the merchant if current process works.
- "Can it answer customers automatically?": answer no for the current release; that integration is planned and not active.
- "Can I pay now?": answer no; subscription checkout is not active.

## Early validation gates
Before spending on ads, aim for 10 structured merchant interviews, 5 supervised pilots, and at least 3 merchants willing to pay after the production product is available. Track activation, weekly use, support time, retention, gross margin and churn. These are validation targets, not forecasts.
