# MAVORA pricing decision — Morocco first

Date: 2026-11-27
Status: recommended launch hypotheses; not activated for sale.

## Competitive observations
Publicly displayed Moroccan prices reviewed on 2026-11-27:
- Nadigit IMS: Starter 99 MAD/month, Pro 299 MAD/month, Business 547 MAD/month.
- Kiwi: Basic 150 MAD/month (or 99 MAD/month on annual billing), Plus 300 MAD/month, Pro 399 MAD/month.
- Side: Essentiel 149 MAD/month, Business 199 MAD/month, Premium 299 MAD/month.
- Tijarato: Solo 99 MAD/month, Business 199 MAD/month, Pro 399 MAD/month, Network 699 MAD/month.
- ManagePro: Essential 199 MAD/month, Commerce 349 MAD/month, Business 499 MAD/month (excluding tax, per its pricing page).
- PosXT: 299 / 499 / 799 MAD per month excluding tax.

These are advertised prices and feature bundles, not audited sales or proof of customer willingness to pay. Feature sets differ materially.

## Proposed MAVORA launch prices
| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Included limits (initial proposal) |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Starter | 79 MAD | 790 MAD | 1 user, 500 products, catalog, stock, orders, export, email support |
| Pro | 149 MAD | 1,490 MAD | 3 users, 3,000 products, 200 AI replies/month once live, advanced reports |
| Business | 299 MAD | 2,990 MAD | 10 users, 10,000 products, 1,000 AI replies/month once live, priority support, WhatsApp connection when approved |

Annual billing is 10 months' price (two months free). AI replies are capped; overages require explicit top-up or upgrade. Meta/WhatsApp platform message fees are passed through separately and are not included in plan price.

## Setup service
- 299 MAD one-time: import/clean up to 100 product records and one remote onboarding session.
- 499 MAD one-time: up to 300 product records and onboarding.
- Above 300 products, image editing, manual data repair, on-site work or custom integrations: quote separately.

Do not promise an unlimited setup service at a flat fee. Count labor and scope.

## Why revise 49 / 99 / 199 MAD
Those initial prices were unsupported guesses and did not account for AI inference, support labor, onboarding, database/backup costs, WhatsApp charges, billing fees, refunds, taxes or merchant support. The new price points are closer to observed local alternatives while keeping MAVORA below mature POS/ERP offerings because the product is not yet production-ready.

## Validation gates
1. Interview at least 10 electronics/accessory shops.
2. Run 5 supervised pilots with test/approved data.
3. Obtain at least 3 explicit paid-pilot commitments before spending on acquisition.
4. Measure support minutes per store, AI cost per answer, WhatsApp cost per active customer, gross margin, activation and 30/60-day retention.
5. Revisit prices after actual cost and conversion data; do not treat this as a final price list.

## International pricing
Do not simply convert Moroccan MAD prices into USD. Start international tests only after product-market fit, using localized price overrides and separate unit economics. A possible first test is USD 9 / 19 / 39 monthly, subject to costs and willingness-to-pay research. It is not yet published as a live offer.
